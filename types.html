Types of Cyberbullying

Types of Cyberbullying

😤

Harassment

Repeated, unwanted contact including mean messages, insults, and threats.

🎭

Impersonation

Creating fake accounts pretending to be someone else to damage their reputation.

🔍

Doxxing

Sharing private information like address or phone number.

😠

Hate Speech

Content targeting based on race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.

⚡

Threats & Intimidation

Threatening violence or harm against someone or their loved ones.

👥

Exclusion & Ostracism

Intentionally leaving someone out of group activities or chats.

📢

Rumor Spreading

Spreading false or embarrassing information to damage reputation.

📸

Outing & Trickery

Sharing private information or tricking someone into sharing secrets.
